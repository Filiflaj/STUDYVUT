# Manual

## Dashboard {#dashboard}

Pick your **programme** and **year** at the top: the Dashboard, the sidebar and the subject menus of Study and Quizzes then show only your subjects. The choice is remembered.

- **Subject tiles**, grouped by term: how many chapters and questions a subject has and how many questions are due. Click a tile to open the subject's page.

## Subject page {#subject}

Pick what to do in the subject: **Study**, **Quizzes**, **Assessment** (how to pass it) and **Submitted work**, plus **Review** when questions are due. Study and Quizzes have a link back at the top.

## Study {#study}

The subject's chapters are on the left; the open chapter also lists its sections, highlighted as you read. Change the subject at the top right or in the sidebar.

The texts have boxes: **Zapamatuj si** (remember this), **Pozor** (a common mistake), **Příklad** (a worked example) and **Poznámka** (a note). Figures follow the light and dark theme; click a photo or chart to enlarge it. Text can be selected and copied.

Under a chapter, **Practise this chapter** starts a quiz on its topics; the arrows open the previous and next chapter.

## Quizzes {#quizzes}

At the top are the subject's quiz sets with their size: the subject's own sets (such as old tests or a mock exam), one per topic, **My mistakes** and **Due for review**.

Click a set to start it. Answer with the mouse or the keys <kbd>a</kbd>–<kbd>h</kbd> (or <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>8</kbd>); <kbd>Enter</kbd> goes on. For numeric questions type the number (decimal comma or point) and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>; if only the sign is wrong, the app says so.

After each answer you see what was right, **why your answer is wrong**, the explanation and a note on every option. At the end: the result, the wrong answers and **Repeat the wrong ones**. A quiz waits while you visit other pages; **End quiz** closes it, the answers so far stay recorded.

## Spaced repetition {#repetition}

Every question you answer gets a plan: after a right answer it comes back after 1, then 2, 4, 8 and finally 16 days; a wrong answer starts it over at one day. Days follow your calendar, so a question from the evening is back the next morning.

## Settings {#settings}

Appearance and language (the study material is Czech either way), which subjects to show, where the content comes from and whether it is valid, your data folder, exporting every answer as JSON, resetting progress, the version and **What's new**.

## Your data {#data}

Progress and settings stay on your computer, in the folder you open from Settings → Your data; nothing is sent anywhere. New versions keep your progress: questions have fixed ids, so added or corrected material never erases it.
